# Phase 15B: source-rights and acquisition feasibility

Research date 2026-10-25. Nothing was ingested, fetched in bulk, cached or stored: only terms pages and one-line search results were read. Each row separates **what was read** from **what could not be verified**. A source whose terms were not read is treated as **restricted** until a person reviews it; the existing gates (`research/history/sources.json`, `scripts/history/lib/permissions.mjs`, the hosted `source_publication_guard`) stay in force. A per-use grant is needed for fetch, local processing and site publication, separately.

## What follows
1. **No cleared, free source covers 2023 or 2024 transactions.** Retrosheet's file ends in 2020, Lahman has no transactions, and everything current is MLBAM, Sports Reference or a paid/unknown-terms publisher. The plan therefore cannot rest on a bulk free feed.
2. **Contracts have no cleared source at all.** Every rich contract source is paid, unread, or restricted. Contract accounting (capabilities 3 and 4) is blocked until a rights decision or a license.
3. **History before 2021 is feasible** from Retrosheet (with its credit line) for trades and movements, and from Lahman/Chadwick for identity, manager tenures and old salaries. That supports historical trade reconstruction (no contract terms) after a hosted attribution category and credit surface are approved, the same pending step as for the draft work (`docs/history/retrosheet-and-wikidata.md`).
4. **Decisions the owner needs to make, in order of value:**
   - **(a)** Request written MLBAM authorization (use the existing outreach drafts in `docs/history/license-outreach-drafts.md`) for non-bulk, cited transaction facts, stated per use: fetch, local processing, site publication. Cheapest unlock for 2023-2024 trades and releases.
   - **(b)** Ask one licensed vendor for a written quote and the display/redistribution terms. Needed for contracts at any scale.
   - **(c)** Approve a Retrosheet attribution category and credit surface for hosted publication (historical trades).
   - **(d)** Confirm Lahman's license from the download's readme before deciding whether share-alike is acceptable.
5. **Until then:** the pilot (`05`) is restricted to individually sourced facts, each cited to an independent, non-bulk page, with sources that are not cleared for publication kept local and never committed (the repository is public).
6. **Unverified here, to confirm before relying on them:** the current Sports Reference, Spotrac, Pro Sports Transactions, FanGraphs and MLB Trade Rumors terms; the Lahman license text and whether a current Salaries table exists; which years the MLB wire exposes; any vendor price.
