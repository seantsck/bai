# 1965 pilot: a lawful-source feasibility plan (planning only)

Nothing here starts a transcription project, imports the restricted MLB pilot, or scrapes a commercial archive. The 1965 selections already in the local research database come from restricted sources and stay there; this plan describes how a publishable 1965 could be built without them.

## Phases
| Phase | Expected size | Notes |
|---|---|---|
| June regular phase (June 8, 1965) | on the order of 800–830 picks (the stated total is itself disputed: Wikipedia's article says 813; it cites a newspaper figure of 826) | The first draft. Two sources disagree on the total, so no expected count is claimed until a primary list settles it. |
| August American Legion phase | small (tens of picks; the count is not known) | Reported separately and much less widely. Retrosheet's file marks Legion-draft rows (draft type L). Kept as its own class; never mixed into June. |
| January and June secondary phases | none that year | Secondary phases began later; confirm from the lists rather than assume. |

## Lawful sources
- **Contemporary newspapers (AP/UPI lists, June 9–10, 1965):** the facts (who was picked, by which club, in what order) are not copyrightable (*Feist*); U.S. issues from before 1978 printed without a copyright notice are public domain. Counsel should confirm this reading before transcription starts.
- **Access without bulk extraction:** library microfilm and public-library newspaper databases read by a person, one page at a time. Commercial archive terms (Newspapers.com, GenealogyBank and similar) generally forbid scraping or bulk download, so those are used only as a viewer, never harvested. Chronicling America stops at 1963 and does not help.
- **Corroboration and identity:** Retrosheet (138 draft records for 1965, all people it tracks), the Chadwick register (ids, birth dates) and Lahman-style biographical tables for crosswalking. Wikipedia's article supplies the stated total with a revision id.
- **Excluded:** MLB's draft feed and Baseball-Reference pages (restricted), the Baseball America Ultimate Draft Book and The Baseball Cube register (copyrighted; a written license would change this, see the outreach drafts), Sporting News guides (1964–77 copyrights were renewed automatically; facts only, after legal review).

## Effort
| Task | Hours (estimate) |
|---|---:|
| Locate and photograph/read two independent complete lists (AP and UPI, or two papers) | 6–10 |
| Double-keyed transcription of about 830 June picks at about 60 per hour, two people | 28 |
| Reconcile the two keyings (typical disagreement 3–5%) and per-club counts | 4–6 |
| Legion phase: locate and transcribe | 3–6 |
| Identity crosswalk (names vs Chadwick and Retrosheet; most picks never played, so most stay name-only) and exception review | 8–12 |
| **Total** | **about 50–65 person-hours; cash cost $0–500 (copies, subscription month)** |

## Independent verification
1. Two separate newspaper lists of the same phase, keyed independently; every disagreement is an exception, never silently resolved.
2. Per-club counts compared across the two lists; a club is a COMPLETE_CENSUS only when both lists give the same people and nothing is held. Otherwise PARTIAL_RECONSTRUCTION.
3. Retrosheet's 1965 records (including its "drafted, did not sign" rows) checked against the lists: each Retrosheet draftee should appear; a miss is a finding.
4. The stated total settled from a primary account, not from either aggregator.
5. Signing status stays UNKNOWN unless a separate source states it.

## Gates before any work
- Counsel's confirmation of the *Feist*/no-notice analysis, recorded in the source catalog as a new `contemporary-newspapers` entry (AUTHORIZED or CLEARED, with the basis) by a person.
- A decision whether a license from Baseball America or The Baseball Cube would be cheaper than 50–65 hours.
- A reviewer with their own database login (docs/decision-history.md) for the exceptions.
